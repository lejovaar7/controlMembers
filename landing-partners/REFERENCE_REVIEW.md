# Partner landing: reference and copy review

Reviewed on 2026-09-22 using the companies' own published pages. These are
references for reseller/affiliate programs, not claims that their products or
commercial terms are identical to ControlMembers.

| Reference | Relevant sections on its page | Application to this landing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Alegra Partners](https://www.alegra.com/partners/) | Program explanation, benefits, partner profiles, application steps, contact | Explain who can sell the software and replace vague promotion with concrete steps. |
| [Holded Solution Partners](https://www.holded.com/es/solution-partners) | Program benefits, recurring commissions, requirements, testimonials, FAQ, application | State the partner's role and make commission conditions easy to find. |
| [Gymkee Affiliate Program](https://gymkee.com/coach/affiliate/) | Earnings calculator, recurring commission conditions, milestones, three joining steps, customer testimonials | Keep the calculator prominent, with clear inputs and a distinction between monthly and annual estimates. Gymkee serves fitness coaches, an adjacent market. |

The pages also advertise benefits specific to their own programs. This landing
does not adopt their certifications, free registration, partner dashboards,
tracking links, bonuses, testimonials, support promises, or payment schedules.
Those require confirmed ControlMembers policies and actual supporting services.
Competitor wording has not been copied into the landing.

## Content decisions

- Replace ambiguous sales uses of "cartera" with "clientes" or "clientes
  asociados a tu nombre". Describe actual product receivables as pending balances.
- State what is sold, to whom, and when commission is generated in the hero.
- Replace motivational headings with specific labels: monthly commission,
  product functions, partner profiles, joining steps, and common questions.
- Add a short partner-profile section so prospective sellers can identify
  whether the program is relevant to them.
- Describe only implemented product functions: pupils, enrollments, monthly
  charges, payment records, balances, reports, branches, and manual WhatsApp drafts.
- Keep the three proposed price scenarios and adjustable 30% simulation.
- Explain that commission accrual on monthly customer payments is distinct from
  the agreed payout schedule for the partner.
- Keep conditions that are not finalized explicit. Do not invent a payout date,
  refund policy, guaranteed income, or automatic referral attribution.

## Brand source

The header, footer, and favicon use a byte-for-byte copy of
`src/react-app/assets/controlmembers-logo.svg`, the same SVG imported by the
platform's `ProductBrand` component. It is served locally and retains its colors
and proportions. The separate program label is not part of the logo artwork.
