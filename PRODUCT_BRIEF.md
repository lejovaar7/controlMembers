# ControlMembers Product Brief

ControlMembers is a multi-tenant SaaS for organizations that manage recurring
member fees. It is designed for sports, music, martial arts, swimming, dance,
language and similar academies without encoding any one discipline into the
core product.

The detailed MVP contract lives in [`specs/`](specs/README.md). Delivery order,
user stories and open product decisions live in [`planning/`](planning/README.md).
Verification evidence remains in [`specs/VERIFICATION.md`](specs/VERIFICATION.md).
